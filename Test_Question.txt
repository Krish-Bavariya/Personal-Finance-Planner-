🧪 Finance Agent Test Questions
📊 Basic Data Retrieval (tests data-grounding)
1.  What is my total spending and how many transactions do I have?
2  .Which category did I spend the most on?
3.  Show me a breakdown of my spending by category with percentages.
📅 Time-Based Analysis (tests monthly grouping & trends)
4.  Which month had the highest spending and by how much?
5  .How has my spending changed month over month?
6.  What was my average monthly expenditure?
🔍 Deep Dives (tests analytical depth)
7.  What are my top 5 most expensive single transactions?
8.  Which category has the highest number of transactions but not the highest total spend?
9  .  What percentage of my budget goes to needs vs wants?
💡 Insight & Pattern Detection (tests inference ability)
10.  Are there any unusual or suspicious spending spikes in my data?
11.  Which spending categories are essential vs discretionary?
12.  If I cut my top spending category by 20%, how much would I save per month?
🔗 Multi-Turn / Follow-up (tests conversation context)
13.  (Ask Q3 first, then ask this) → Now compare the top 2 categories from that list.
14.  (Ask Q4 first, then ask this) → What drove the spending in that month? Break it down by category.
15.  (Ask Q12 first, then ask this) → What if I cut that same category by 30% instead?
